# 🪔 Diwali Sales Data Analysis – Business Insights from Consumer Trends

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Diwali season sales data to uncover valuable business insights. The goal is to understand customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and regional trends, enabling businesses to optimize marketing and product strategies during festive campaigns.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To derive meaningful insights from Diwali season sales data, helping businesses:
- Identify key customer segments
- Analyze purchasing behavior
- Optimize regional and category-based marketing strategies

---

## 🧪 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### 👩‍🦰 Gender-Based Insights
- **Female customers** outperformed male customers in both number of purchases and total sales.
- The purchasing power of **women** was significantly higher during the festive season.

### 👥 Age Group Analysis
- Majority of purchases came from customers aged **26–35**, especially **females**.
- This age group is a **prime target** for future festive marketing.

### 🗺️ State-Wise Performance
- **Top states by number of orders**: Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka.
- **Top states by total sales**: Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra.
- **Uttar Pradesh (UP)** emerged as the **highest-contributing state** in both orders and revenue.

### 💍 Marital Status
- **Married individuals**, especially **women**, showed higher buying tendencies and spending.
- Likely influenced by **marriage-related festive purchases**.

### 💼 Occupation Analysis
- Highest number of buyers were from:
  - **IT**
  - **Healthcare**
  - **Aviation**
- These sectors showed the **strongest purchasing power**.

### 📦 Product Category Trends
Top-selling categories:
- **Food**
- **Clothing**
- **Electronics**

These dominated both in **purchase volume** and **revenue generation**.

---

## 💡 Final Insights & Business Recommendations

### 👤 Ideal Customer Persona
- **Married women**, aged **26–35**
- Located in **Uttar Pradesh**, **Maharashtra**, or **Karnataka**
- Working in **IT**, **Healthcare**, or **Aviation**
- Purchasing mainly **Food**, **Clothing**, and **Electronics**

### 📈 Key Recommendations
- Run **targeted campaigns** towards women aged **26–35**
- Offer **special deals** on **food, clothing, and electronics** during festive seasons
- Focus **regional promotions** on **UP, Maharashtra, and Karnataka** for maximum ROI
- Use **occupational segmentation** to offer **customized bundles or discounts**
